Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information when you use Babylu: AI Baby Photos & Videos (the “App”).

By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with the terms of this policy, please do not use the App.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the data needed to provide the App’s features (creating AI baby photos and videos), manage subscriptions and tokens, keep the App stable, and improve the user experience. The App does not require you to create an account.

1.1 Photos You Provide

To create AI-generated baby photos and videos, you choose photos from your photo library or take a photo with your camera. These photos usually contain faces.

  • Photos you upload to create a result
  • AI-generated photos and videos created from your photos

Photos are only accessed when you select them. The App does not access the rest of your photo library. You should only upload photos of yourself or of people who have given you permission to use their photos. Photos of babies or children may only be uploaded by their parent or legal guardian.

1.2 Face Data

Uploaded photos are sent to our servers and to our AI processing providers solely to generate the result you requested. We want to be clear about how faces in your photos are handled:

  • We do not use facial recognition to identify you or anyone else.
  • We do not create or store biometric identifiers, face templates, or face prints.
  • Face data is not used for advertising, marketing, profiling, or tracking.
  • Face data is not sold and is not shared with third parties except the AI processing providers needed to create your result.
  • We do not use your photos, face data, or results to train AI models.

Our AI processing providers may automatically screen uploaded photos and requests with their own safety filters to block prohibited content. Photos are processed by automated systems; we do not manually review them except when needed to provide support you request, investigate abuse, or meet legal obligations.

1.3 Your Consent to AI Processing

The App’s core feature uses third-party artificial intelligence (AI) services. When you select or take a photo and start a creation, that photo (which may include faces) and the selected style instructions are sent to our servers and to the third-party AI processing providers listed in Section 4, solely to create the result you requested.

By uploading a photo and starting a creation, you explicitly consent to this processing and confirm that you have the right and permission to upload the photos of every person shown in them. If a photo shows a baby or child, you confirm that you are their parent or legal guardian and consent to this processing on their behalf. If you do not consent, please do not upload photos or use the creation features. You can withdraw your consent at any time by no longer uploading photos, and you can ask us to delete photos already stored on our servers (see Section 9).

1.4 App Identifier and Device Information

When you use the App, we automatically collect limited technical information:

  • An anonymous app user ID generated by our subscription provider (not linked to your name or email). This ID is stored in your device’s keychain so that your purchases and token balance can be restored, and it may remain after you reinstall the App.
  • Device model, operating system and version, app version, and language
  • Your device region setting and App Store country (used for pricing and localization, not to locate you)
  • Your IP address, which is processed by our servers and providers to deliver the service.
  • Approximate (coarse) location, such as country or city, which our analytics provider (Google Firebase Analytics) infers from your IP address for aggregated usage statistics. We do not request location permission and we never collect your precise location.
  • Number of app sessions and the time of your last app open
  • A push notification token, only if you allow notifications

1.5 Usage, Creation, and Feedback Data

  • Which styles or categories you use and when you create a result
  • Feedback you give on results (for example, like or dislike)
  • App interactions such as screens viewed and buttons pressed (analytics)

1.6 Purchases, Subscriptions, and Tokens

The App offers auto-renewing subscriptions and in-app token packs. Payments are processed by Apple. We do not receive or store your payment card or billing details.

  • Subscription status, plan, and subscription period dates
  • Purchase transaction identifiers for token packs
  • Your token balance and token usage history
  • Fraud and abuse signals, such as unusually frequent requests, daily usage limits reached, and refunds

1.7 Crash and Performance Data

  • Crash reports and diagnostic logs
  • Performance metrics such as launch time and responsiveness

1.8 Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect:

  • Name, email address, phone number, or physical address
  • Contacts
  • Precise location (GPS); the App does not request location permission
  • Payment card information
  • Advertising identifiers (IDFA) used for tracking

2. Tracking and Advertising

  • The App does not show third-party advertising.
  • The App does not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies.
  • The App does not request App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission.

3. How We Use Information

We use the information described above to:

  • Create AI baby photos and videos from the photos you provide
  • Show your creations in the App
  • Manage subscriptions, restore purchases, and keep your token balance accurate
  • Prevent fraud and abuse (for example, duplicate or fake purchase claims)
  • Send notifications, if you have allowed them
  • Measure app usage, fix crashes, and improve features and performance

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data for advertising purposes.

4. Data Sharing and Service Providers

We share limited data only with service providers that help us operate the App:

  • Third-party AI processing providers — receive your uploaded photos and the selected style instructions only to generate your result. These currently include Replicate (which runs AI models from developers such as OpenAI and Google), fal.ai, and KIE.ai. The provider used may vary by style. They process your photos on our behalf and are not permitted by us to use them for any other purpose.
  • RevenueCat — manages subscriptions and in-app purchases (anonymous app user ID, purchase and subscription information).
  • Apple — processes payments for subscriptions and in-app purchases.
  • Google Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics) — usage analytics, crash reporting, and notifications.
  • Hetzner (servers located in the EU) and Cloudflare (file storage and delivery) — host our servers and store uploaded photos and results on our behalf.

These providers process data on our behalf and according to their own privacy policies and applicable laws. We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights and the safety of our users.

5. Data Storage and Retention

  • Uploaded photos and generated results are stored on our servers only so that we can deliver your results and provide support. They are automatically deleted from our servers within 7 days after creation, or sooner if you ask us to delete them. A basic record of the creation (style, time, status, and tokens used) without any photos is kept for support, accounting, and fraud prevention.
  • Server request logs (which may include your anonymous app user ID and device information) are automatically deleted after 14 days.
  • Your creations are also saved on your device. You can delete them from the App at any time; deleting the App removes them from your device.
  • Subscription, purchase, and token records are kept as long as needed to provide the service and to meet legal, accounting, and fraud-prevention requirements.
  • Analytics and crash data are retained according to Google Firebase’s retention settings.

6. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) for data sent between the App and our servers. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. International Data Transfers

Our service providers may process data in countries other than your own. When we transfer data, we take steps to ensure it remains protected in accordance with this Privacy Policy and applicable laws.

8. Children’s Privacy

The App is intended for adults – parents, guardians and expecting parents – and is not directed to children. Children should not use the App themselves.

  • Parents or legal guardians may upload photos of their own babies or children to create the results they request. Such photos are used only to create those results, are never used for advertising, profiling or AI model training, and are automatically deleted from our servers within 7 days, together with the generated results.
  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children for any other purpose, and we do not ask for a child’s name, age or any other identifying details.
  • If you believe a photo of a child was uploaded without the consent of their parent or legal guardian, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

9. Your Privacy Rights and Choices

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR in the EU/UK or the CCPA in California), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your data, and to object to or restrict certain processing.

  • Delete your photos and data: email us and we will delete the photos and generated results stored on our servers, along with associated data, unless we must keep certain records by law.
  • Notifications: you can turn notifications off at any time in your device settings.
  • Permissions: you can manage photo library and camera access in your device settings.
  • Subscriptions: you can manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple ID settings.
  • Stop collection: you can stop all data collection by uninstalling the App.

We do not sell personal information, as defined under the CCPA.
