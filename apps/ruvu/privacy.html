Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information when you use Ruvu: AI Couple Photos (the “App”).

By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with the terms of this policy, please do not use the App.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the data needed to provide the App’s features (creating AI couple photos and videos), manage subscriptions and tokens, keep the App stable, and improve the user experience. The App does not require you to create an account.

1.1 Photos You Provide

To create AI-generated couple photos and videos, you choose photos from your photo library or take a photo with your camera. These photos usually contain faces.

  • Photos you upload to create a result
  • AI-generated photos and videos created from your photos

Photos are only accessed when you select them. The App does not access the rest of your photo library. You should only upload photos of yourself or of people who have given you permission to use their photos.

1.2 Face Data

Uploaded photos are sent to our servers and to our AI processing providers solely to generate the result you requested. We want to be clear about how faces in your photos are handled:

  • We do not use facial recognition to identify you or anyone else.
  • We do not create or store biometric identifiers, face templates, or face prints.
  • Face data is not used for advertising, marketing, profiling, or tracking.
  • Face data is not sold and is not shared with third parties except the AI processing providers needed to create your result.
  • Face data is not used to train AI models.

1.3 App Identifier and Device Information

When you use the App, we automatically collect limited technical information:

  • An anonymous app user ID generated by our subscription provider (not linked to your name or email)
  • Device model, operating system and version, app version, and language
  • Number of app sessions and the time of your last app open
  • A push notification token, only if you allow notifications

1.4 Usage, Creation, and Feedback Data

  • Which styles or categories you use and when you create a result
  • Feedback you give on results (for example, like or dislike)
  • App interactions such as screens viewed and buttons pressed (analytics)

1.5 Purchases, Subscriptions, and Tokens

The App offers auto-renewing subscriptions and in-app token packs. Payments are processed by Apple. We do not receive or store your payment card or billing details.

  • Subscription status, plan, and subscription period dates
  • Purchase transaction identifiers for token packs
  • Your token balance and token usage history

1.6 Crash and Performance Data

  • Crash reports and diagnostic logs
  • Performance metrics such as launch time and responsiveness

1.7 Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect:

  • Name, email address, phone number, or physical address
  • Contacts
  • Precise or approximate location
  • Payment card information
  • Advertising identifiers (IDFA) used for tracking

2. Tracking and Advertising

  • The App does not show third-party advertising.
  • The App does not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies.
  • The App does not request App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission.

3. How We Use Information

We use the information described above to:

  • Create AI couple photos and videos from the photos you provide
  • Show your creations in the App
  • Manage subscriptions, restore purchases, and keep your token balance accurate
  • Prevent fraud and abuse (for example, duplicate or fake purchase claims)
  • Send notifications, if you have allowed them
  • Measure app usage, fix crashes, and improve features and performance

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data for advertising purposes.

4. Data Sharing and Service Providers

We share limited data only with service providers that help us operate the App:

  • AI processing providers — receive your uploaded photos only to generate your result.
  • RevenueCat — manages subscriptions and in-app purchases (anonymous app user ID, purchase and subscription information).
  • Apple — processes payments for subscriptions and in-app purchases.
  • Google Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics) — usage analytics, crash reporting, and notifications.
  • Hosting and cloud infrastructure providers — store data on our behalf.

These providers process data on our behalf and according to their own privacy policies and applicable laws. We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights and the safety of our users.

5. Data Storage and Retention

  • Uploaded photos and generated results are stored on our servers so that we can deliver your results and provide support. They are kept only as long as necessary for these purposes, or until you ask us to delete them.
  • Your creations are also saved on your device. You can delete them from the App at any time; deleting the App removes them from your device.
  • Subscription, purchase, and token records are kept as long as needed to provide the service and to meet legal, accounting, and fraud-prevention requirements.
  • Analytics and crash data are retained according to Google Firebase’s retention settings.

6. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) for data sent between the App and our servers. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. International Data Transfers

Our service providers may process data in countries other than your own. When we transfer data, we take steps to ensure it remains protected in accordance with this Privacy Policy and applicable laws.

8. Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with data, please contact us and we will delete it.

9. Your Privacy Rights and Choices

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR in the EU/UK or the CCPA in California), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your data, and to object to or restrict certain processing.

  • Delete your photos and data: email us and we will delete the photos and generated results stored on our servers, along with associated data, unless we must keep certain records by law.
  • Notifications: you can turn notifications off at any time in your device settings.
  • Permissions: you can manage photo library and camera access in your device settings.
  • Subscriptions: you can manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple ID settings.
  • Stop collection: you can stop all data collection by uninstalling the App.

We do not sell personal information, as defined under the CCPA.
